Every prompt below is **self-contained** — paste one block into Gemini and go. Global settings (aspect ratio, likeness anchor, negative prompt, technical suffix) are already baked into each block.

---

## Global settings (baked into every prompt)

- **Aspect ratio:** 3:4 portrait
- **Likeness anchor:** A man resembling Christopher Walken: silver slicked-back hair, deep-set pale eyes, gaunt angular face, late-60s or early-70s age, distinctive classic features, sharply attentive expression unless otherwise noted
- **Technical suffix:** Portrait 3:4 aspect ratio, highly detailed, professional cinematography, sharp focus, clear subject framing
- **Negative prompt:** deformed face, extra limbs, extra fingers, malformed hands, watermark, text overlay, subject rendered as a young man, subject rendered as a child, distorted features, uncanny valley, low quality

## Mood routing

Each entry is routed to a mood style based on its tags. Priority: funny > uplifting > tender > defiant > dark > ominous > default.

- **uplifting:** Warm golden palette, bright natural sunlight, subject appears joyful or elated, soft focus, optimistic mood, uplifted expression
- **tender:** Soft warm lighting, gentle expression, intimate framing, pastel palette, quiet emotional warmth
- **funny:** Bright saturated palette, playful absurd staging, comic energy, subject has amused or wry expression, light-hearted mood
- **defiant:** Strong directional lighting, high contrast, bold composition, subject determined and confident, powerful stance
- **dark:** Moody chiaroscuro, desaturated cold palette, deep shadows, tension, restrained emotion
- **ominous:** Noir styling, near-monochrome, heavy shadows, unsettling atmosphere, implied threat
- **default:** Cinematic 35mm film grain, dramatic lighting, balanced mood, classic character framing

## Naming convention

Save each generated image as `<id>.jpg` (e.g. `walk-019.jpg`) so the PWA wires images to entries automatically.
